Painting a gift for my Father

His growing personal interest in artworks ranging from painting to sculpture and photography originates from the Royal Academy of Arts in The Hague. In his spare time he loves to paint in bright vibrant positive colours. His subjects are created in a spontaneous and enthusiastic approach. This Methodology is reflected back in his photography. His motivation he finds in frequently visiting Museums, galleries, art fairs and artist around the world.

Free Work

Mac's artistic view on mixing femininity and masculinity into sensuality rather than sexuality. His skills are finding locations and moments by creating compilations into photographs that are timeless. Not only found in his sensual photography but it could be anything which is in his point of view beautiful. His exceptional devotion makes his subjects feel confident to capture the most private moments. The last said counts for all his work , either for editorial , brands , advertising or free work. Mac's spontaneous and enthusiastic manner of work creates a greater perspective towards the viewer. He believes that with his approach you create a positive and fun relationship on the set.
